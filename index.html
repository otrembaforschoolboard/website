My Connection

Why Osakis Matters to Me

I grew up right here in our community, raised in the area between Long Prairie and Osakis. After living elsewhere for a time, my family and I made the deliberate decision to move back here specifically because of the school district.


We wanted our kids to grow up in a place where education and community go hand-in-hand. Today, I want to give back by helping build on the strong base that currently exists at Osakis Public Schools.

My Priorities

Guided by Experience

Facility Build-Out

We are entering a vital phase for our school's physical future. I am excited to help guide us through the new facility build-out, ensuring we create spaces that inspire learning while remaining fiscally responsible to our taxpayers.

Technology & AI

With my professional background in technology, I offer a unique perspective on the challenges and opportunities of Artificial Intelligence in the classroom. I want to help our students and teachers navigate these tools safely and effectively.

Community Legacy

Our schools are the heart of Osakis. I am committed to maintaining the high standards we moved here for, ensuring that every Silverstreak student has the foundation they need to succeed in a rapidly changing world.
